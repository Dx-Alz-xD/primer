How it works

  1. 1

    Name your topic

    Add a range, like “from limits to the chain rule”, and say how you like to be taught.

  2. 2

    AI plans the lessons

    You get a detailed outline, as long as your range needs, grouped into units, in the order to learn them.

  3. 3

    Learn in checkpoints

    Each lesson opens piece by piece as you answer. Get one wrong and it revises with you first.

  4. 4

    Ask about anything

    Select a line or sketch on the whiteboard to ask a doubt right where it came up.
